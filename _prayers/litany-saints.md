### Our Family's Patrons

Holy Mary, Mother of God and our Mother, *pray for us.*<br>
St. Michael the Archangel, my namesake and defender in battle, *pray for us.*<br>
St. Joseph, husband, father, protector, and worker, *pray for us.*<br>
St. Peter, Prince of the Apostles, *pray for us.*<br>
St. Paul, my patron, *pray for us.*<br>
St. Jude, helper in things despaired of, *pray for us.*<br>
St. Matthias, patron of our son, *pray for us.*<br>
St. Blaise, guardian of our children's breath, *pray for us.*<br>
St. Patrick, of Ireland and of San Francisco, *pray for us.*<br>
St. Richard, patron of my father, *pray for us.*<br>
St. Dominic, in whose church we met, were married, and baptized Matthias, *pray for us.*<br>
St. Francis, patron of our city, where we met, *pray for us.*<br>
St. Gerard, patron of mothers and of those who long for children, *pray for us.*<br>
St. Agatha, patron of Lisa's family, *pray for us.*<br>
St. Lucy, guardian of my eyes, *pray for us.*<br>
St. Cecilia, patron of the music in our lives, *pray for us.*<br>
St. Anastasia, patron of the Brennan family, *pray for us.*<br>
St. Elizabeth of Hungary, patron of Lisa, *pray for us.*<br>
St. Rita, patron of my mother, *pray for us.*

Be merciful, *spare us, O Lord.*<br>
Be merciful, *graciously hear us, O Lord.*

From all evil, *deliver us, O Lord.*<br>
From all sin, *deliver us, O Lord.*<br>
From Your wrath, *deliver us, O Lord.*<br>
From a sudden and unprovided death, *deliver us, O Lord.*<br>
From the snares of the devil, *deliver us, O Lord.*<br>
From anger, hatred, and all ill will, *deliver us, O Lord.*<br>
From the spirit of fornication, *deliver us, O Lord.*<br>
From lightning and tempest, *deliver us, O Lord.*<br>
From the scourge of earthquake, *deliver us, O Lord.*<br>
From plague, famine, and war, *deliver us, O Lord.*<br>
From everlasting death, *deliver us, O Lord.*<br>
By the mystery of Your holy Incarnation, *deliver us, O Lord.*<br>
By Your Coming, *deliver us, O Lord.*<br>
By Your Birth, *deliver us, O Lord.*<br>
By Your Baptism and holy fasting, *deliver us, O Lord.*<br>
By Your Cross and Passion, *deliver us, O Lord.*<br>
By Your Death and Burial, *deliver us, O Lord.*<br>
By Your holy Resurrection, *deliver us, O Lord.*<br>
By Your wonderful Ascension, *deliver us, O Lord.*<br>
By the coming of the Holy Spirit, the Paraclete, *deliver us, O Lord.*<br>
On the day of judgment, *deliver us, O Lord.*

We sinners, *we beseech You, hear us.*<br>
That You spare us, *we beseech You, hear us.*<br>
That You pardon us, *we beseech You, hear us.*<br>
That You bring us to true penance, *we beseech You, hear us.*<br>
That You govern and preserve Your holy Church, *we beseech You, hear us.*<br>
That You preserve our Holy Father and all orders of the Church in holy religion, *we beseech You, hear us.*<br>
That You humble the enemies of holy Church, *we beseech You, hear us.*<br>
That You give peace and true concord to all Christian rulers, *we beseech You, hear us.*<br>
That You grant peace and unity to all Christian peoples, *we beseech You, hear us.*<br>
That You recall to the unity of the Church all who have strayed, and lead all unbelievers to the light of the Gospel, *we beseech You, hear us.*<br>
That You confirm and preserve us in Your holy service, *we beseech You, hear us.*<br>
That You lift up our minds to heavenly desires, *we beseech You, hear us.*<br>
That You render eternal blessings to all our benefactors, *we beseech You, hear us.*<br>
That You deliver our souls, and those of our brethren, relatives, and benefactors, from eternal damnation, *we beseech You, hear us.*<br>
That You give and preserve the fruits of the earth, *we beseech You, hear us.*<br>
That You grant eternal rest to all the faithful departed, *we beseech You, hear us.*<br>
That You bless our marriage and grant us the gift of children, *we beseech You, hear us.*<br>
That You heal and strengthen our children, *we beseech You, hear us.*<br>
That You keep our parents and our families in Your grace, *we beseech You, hear us.*<br>
That You graciously hear us, *we beseech You, hear us.*<br>
Son of God, *we beseech You, hear us.*

Lamb of God, who take away the sins of the world, *spare us, O Lord.*<br>
Lamb of God, who take away the sins of the world, *graciously hear us, O Lord.*<br>
Lamb of God, who take away the sins of the world, *have mercy on us.*

Christ, hear us.<br>
℟. Christ, graciously hear us.

Lord, have mercy on us.<br>
℟. Christ, have mercy on us.<br>
Lord, have mercy on us.

**Let us pray.** Almighty ever-living God, by whose gift we venerate in one celebration the merits of all the Saints, bestow on us, we pray, through the prayers of so many intercessors, an abundance of the reconciliation with You for which we earnestly long. Through our Lord Jesus Christ, Your Son, who lives and reigns with You in the unity of the Holy Spirit, one God, for ever and ever. Amen.
